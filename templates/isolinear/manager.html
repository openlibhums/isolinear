About Isolinear

When enabled, this plugin allows editors to publish article manuscripts as preprints in the linked repository directly from the editorial workflow. Subsequent revisions and peer reviews are incorporated automatically.

  • Publish a manuscript as a preprint from the editor assignment page
  • Create new preprint versions as revisions are submitted
  • View and manage all preprints from the preprint list
